Status: complete; LW-4C2 may begin

## Scope and ownership result

LW-4C1 did not change the accepted lifecycle, public semantic families, execution-family algebra,
KernelAbstractions ordering contract or backend qualification model. It introduced private,
package-owned descriptions for facts that were previously reconstructed independently by each
lowering:

- exact topology epoch/count/item-domain and dense-route structure;
- immutable binding requirements, including access and layout;
- static topology payload selection, device copying and checked transfer bytes;
- algorithmic workspace leaves, validation, alias rejection, enumeration and checked bytes;
- the complete eight-dimensional determinism schema and required per-port evidence envelope; and
- typed rank, identity and winner primitives shared only by admitted atomic arbitration paths.

Family code continues to own independent coverage, deterministic/fast numerical laws, semantic-ID
uniqueness, dynamic eligibility, conjunctive two-claim semantics, publication kernels and launch
schedules. Inspection descriptors do not choose executable code.

## Specialization disposition

The direct one-launch, general buffered, two-launch single-resolved and four-launch conjunctive
paths remain justified by their distinct semantics or measured performance.

The legacy four-launch `_ResolvedWinnerLowering` remains isolated. Generic resolved work proves
the accepted rank/identity/empty semantics and is the public cross-domain/performance path, but it
does not yet preserve the legacy named-family operation descriptor, eager `masked` declaration,
flat topology shape, winner-table inspection or caller workspace shape without a second adapter.
Deleting that path during C1 would therefore either break an existing authoring surface or add a
new normalization wrapper with duplicated lifecycle dispatch. Neither outcome is consolidation.
The path has no CorePotts production authority and must not be used as precedent for a new
mechanism. C3 decides its public deprecation status; LW-5 may not adopt it.

This is the exact C1-08 retained gap. It is not a claim that the specialization has a performance
advantage.
