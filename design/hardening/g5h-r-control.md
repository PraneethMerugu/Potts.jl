## Control rules

- Research artifacts cannot be cited as capability or product qualification.
- Production source, exports, Merks, stable manual claims, and passed capability rows remain frozen
  until an amendment clears committee fidelity review and owner ratification.
- Committee members did not author the research packet or the proposed production implementation.
- Every state transition records an exact commit/tree and the commands required to reproduce it.
- Existing G5H gates are marked `reopened` only by the accepted post-review amendment, never by a
  research hypothesis.
