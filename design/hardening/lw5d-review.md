## Contradiction/red-team round

Each reviewer received the other memos' findings only after all independent
memos were complete. The round concentrated on the forged private combined
profile and statistical evidence retention.

All three agreed that the combined state is a real defensive-validation gap:
generic preflight validates its two capacities independently, enqueue could
append both prefixes, and settlement has no reviewed combined completion
method. The correct future repair is a cold-path/preflight rejection, not a
combined settlement implementation. They retained it as P2 because no
supported initializer, selector, restore, adaptation, checkpoint, or host
transaction can construct it; doing so requires deliberate use of private
underscored implementation objects. Promotion does not widen that graph. It
escalates to P1 if any supported construction path becomes reachable.

The round also agreed that missing retained raw paired samples is P2. Exact
hashed benchmark code, protocol, environment, thresholds and aggregate results
make the gate reviewable, but future evidence should retain raw samples to
permit re-bootstrap without rerunning. The private broad `getproperty` shim
also remains P2 maintenance debt.

No reviewer found a contradiction in exact science/RNG/order, one-final-wait,
provider-failure, specialization, allocation, portability, or CorePotts
ownership claims. All emphasized that a production seal is not yet earned:
only the narrow reviewed delta is authorized, followed by complete reruns and
new hashes.
