Scientific case-study boundary

Content class: qualified source-bounded case studies in a qualified unpublished internal beta.

These pages demonstrate how a scientific model can be assembled with the ordinary PottsToolkit and ProcessBigraphs APIs. They show complete source inline. They do not import a prebuilt reference-model constructor, hide scientific setup, or claim to reproduce a paper figure.

How to read the evidence

Each page separates:

  1. source citation and traced mechanism;
  2. accepted reduced profile and deviations from the canonical profile;
  3. complete executable assembly;
  4. typed observations and deterministic figures;
  5. semantic/equivalence checks against the packaged model family;
  6. admitted conclusions and explicit nonclaims.

The paper authors have not endorsed these software reconstructions.
